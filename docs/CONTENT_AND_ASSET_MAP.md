## 3. Conflicts & open questions (not resolved on the site)

1. **Stonir thickness** — Intro catalogue: 1–2 mm (flexible) / 2–4 mm (board). SKU pages: "2 mm · 4 mm". Range page shows the intro values; product pages show the SKU values.
2. **Stonir codes reused across series** — e.g. `SSV 1000/1` appears in Natural *and* Translucent with different stones. Codes are treated as unique **within a collection** only (URLs include the collection).
3. **10x4 codes vs Natural codes** — 10x4 lists "SSV 3000", Natural lists "SSV 3000/1…/4". Kept as separate products in their own collection.
4. **StonIT material** — Intro says "real stone surface bonded to a high-strength FRP backing" and also "hand-painted stone texture with protective coating". Site publishes only the spec-table lines (FRP composition; hand-painted finish) and does **not** claim a real-stone face.
5. **StonIT design descriptions that don't match their designs** — Flora Relief ("3D bar graph"), Maple Arch / Vertical Vista (identical text), Rubik Round, Alpine Peak, Pure Obsidian, Versailles Bloom. Descriptions omitted for these until the client confirms.
6. **StonIT duplicate colour pages** — Maple Arch Black Copper (p.20, p.23), Maple Arch Yellow (p.21, p.26), Petal Porcelain Beige (p.27, p.28). See `finalize_catalog.py` for handling.
7. **StonIT "Fire Retardant" / "Weather Resistant", Stonir "10–15 years durability", ASTM results** — published as stated in the client's own catalogues; no independent verification.
8. **Stonir collections without SKUs** — Metallic, Concrete, Grainy Translucent, Deco Metal Inlay, Deco Louver, 3D Panel are described in the intro but have no shade card; not published as collections.
9. **Stonir intro calls Stonir "a premium surface brand… headquartered in India"** — conflicts with the brief's hierarchy (Stonir = range of Ston-i Surfaces). Site follows the brief.
